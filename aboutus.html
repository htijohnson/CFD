About Our District

Learn about the Coburg Fire District, our mission, personnel, and history serving the community.

Mission & Overview

The Coburg Rural Fire Protection District delivers emergency medical response, fire suppression, technical rescue, and public safety education services to the residents and businesses of Coburg, Oregon, and surrounding rural areas.

Operating from our primary station headquarters, our dedicated staff and volunteer firefighters respond 24/7 to safeguard lives, property, and the local environment.

Our Services & Coverage

  • Structural & Wildland Fire

    Equipped for structural protection, wildland fire containment, and mutual aid support.

  • Emergency Medical Response

    Rapid medical aid providing life support prior to ambulance transport.

  • Fire Prevention & Inspections

    Community safety inspections, burn permits, and defensible space consultations.

  • Rescue & Hazard Mitigation

    Extrication and response to motor vehicle crashes and environmental hazards.
